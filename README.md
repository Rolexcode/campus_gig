# CampusGig

CampusGig is a global freelance platform built around students.

Clients can post work, review applicants and hire verified students, while students can discover paid opportunities, apply for gigs, build a work history and grow their reputation through completed jobs.

The idea is simple: students already have useful skills, but finding trusted paid work can be difficult when they are competing on general freelance platforms. CampusGig creates a space where student talent is the focus.

## How it works

1. A client posts a gig.
2. Verified students apply.
3. The client reviews applicants and selects a student.
4. Both sides agree on the work, amount and deadline.
5. The student completes and submits the work.
6. The client approves the delivery and leaves a review.
7. The completed gig contributes to the student's reputation on the platform.

CampusGig is designed to make it easier for students anywhere to turn their skills into real work experience and income while giving clients a simple way to discover student talent.
