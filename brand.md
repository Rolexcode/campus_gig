# Brand — CampusGig

_Status: active_

CampusGig is a trustworthy gig marketplace for verified students across borders, launching with an Africa-first focus and global reach. Familiar local payment methods keep the product accessible, while optional Solana-powered USDC settlement gives cross-border users a shared rail when it is useful. The product should feel like a dependable fintech service: calm, precise, credible, and practical.

## Palette

- Ink: near-black navy for primary text and navigation
- Canvas: cool off-white for page backgrounds
- Surface: white for cards and forms
- Trust green: restrained deep green for primary actions, verification, and secured-funds moments
- Amber: warnings and pending states only
- Red: rejection and destructive states only

## Typography

- UI: Geist Sans
- Numbers and simulated account details: Geist Mono
- Headlines are confident, compact, and sentence case

## Voice

- Direct and reassuring
- Specific about money and status
- Never playful about escrow, identity, or payment
- African relevance is reflected through real institutions and genuine payment-access problems, without limiting the product to one country
- Global reach is communicated through cross-border campuses, country-aware institutions, flexible settlement, and plain language rather than crypto jargon
